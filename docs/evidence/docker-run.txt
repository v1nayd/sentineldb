======================================================================
ADDENDUM (same day): root cause and fix for the intermittent failure above
======================================================================
Reproduced on the project's own Docker stack (MySQL 8.0.46, host port 3307): 1 failure in 6 runs, then 20 failures in 30
isolated runs of  `vitest run tests/sync.test.ts -t "four workers"`.

Cause: InnoDB deadlocks (errno 1213, "Deadlock found when trying to get lock") between parallel workers. A job-state UPDATE
(finish / fail / requeue) locks the job's primary-key row and then its idx_jobs_claim entry; another worker's claim scan
(SELECT ... FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED over that index) takes the locks in the opposite order. Only the claim transaction retried
on 1213. Consequences seen:
  - the stale-lock recovery UPDATE at the top of claim() threw the deadlock out of runSync (first failure), and
  - a job-state UPDATE deadlocked inside process1, the job was recorded as failed ("Deadlock found..." in last_error)
    and put back to pending with a backoff, so only 11 of 12 jobs were `done` (assertion `expected 11 to be 12`).
    All 12 had been claimed; the assertion that failed was the `done` count (tests/sync.test.ts:165), not `claimed`.

Fix (src/lib/sync.ts): one helper `retryOnDeadlock` (same retry as before: up to 5 attempts, short random backoff) is now used
for the stale-lock recovery, the claim transaction and every job-state UPDATE (finish, fail x2, requeue). Those UPDATEs are
fenced on status = 'running' AND locked_by = <worker>, so repeating one is safe.

Result after the fix, same Docker stack:
  - `four workers` test, isolated: 40 of 40 runs passed (before the fix: 10 of 30).
  - tests/sync + sync-failure + reconcile (23 tests): 8 of 8 runs passed.
  - full suite `npm test`: 230 of 230 passed (24 test files).
